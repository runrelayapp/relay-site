Running is usually something you do alone. Relay changes the pace — your crew can send voice notes, texts, and songs timed to the exact mile or minute you need them, so you're never really running by yourself. Turns out, the miles go by faster with company.

00
Start line

Set up your race in a minute.

Name it, set the date and distance, and choose how messages arrive — GPS-triggered by mile, or timer-triggered by minute, so training runs and treadmill miles get cheered on too.

01
Rally your crew

Share your race in seconds.

Send a link or a QR code and anyone can hop in — your coach, your college roommate, your whole group chat. No account required for them to start cheering.

02
Send relays

Cheer from anywhere.

No app to download — the link opens a simple web form right in their browser. Your crew can record a voice note, write a message, or send a song, then pick the exact mile or minute they want it to land.

03
During the run

Every mile, tracked live.

Distance, pace, and a projected finish time update as you go — so you always know exactly where you are and how much course is left.

04
Voices from the sidelines

Right when you need them.

Messages surface automatically at their mile or minute — no checking your phone mid-stride. If one hits just right, tap to replay it as many times as you need to get you through.

🏁
Finish line

Save the memory, not just the number.

Every race gets saved with the messages that carried you through it — distance, time, and pace alongside the voices that got you to the finish.
